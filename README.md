## About

I am an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at Beihang University. My research focuses on adapting vision-language models to aerial imagery, evidence-grounded visual reasoning, and multimodal perception systems. I am particularly interested in turning research ideas into efficient and deployable solutions for real-world UAV scenarios.

## Selected Research

### ECCV 2026 | DroneFINE

**DroneFINE: Domain-Aware Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning of Vision-Language Detectors for Drone Images**<br>
First author · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00338)

- Formulates UAV-domain adaptation as two complementary challenges: sparse foreground representation and large-area background interference.
- Introduces a foreground-aware HyperAdapter and semantic background suppression to improve parameter-efficient adaptation while retaining practical inference efficiency.

### ACM MM Workshop 2024 | UAV-AdaptiveNet

**Domain Adaptive Object Detection for UAV-based Images by Robust Representation Learning and Multiple Pseudo-label Aggregation**<br>
First author · [Paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3688863.3689576)

- Develops robust cross-domain representation learning and teacher-student multi-pseudo-label aggregation for UAV imagery under domain shift.

### BMVC 2023 | Multimodal Depression Diagnosis

**Integrating Transient and Long-term Physical States for Depression Intelligent Diagnosis**<br>
Co-first author · [Paper](https://papers.bmvc2023.org/0119.pdf) · [Code](https://github.com/Clearlangw/Depression-diagnosis-based-on-EEG-and-Tongue)

- Combines transient EEG states and long-term tongue-image cues through Trans_EEGNet and a ViT-based visual branch for multimodal diagnosis.

## Featured Project

### ARTS 2026 Low-altitude Intelligent Perception Challenge | Visual Grounding

[Competition](https://www.atrdata.cn/#/customer/match) · **A-rank 3rd · B-rank 2nd**

- Built an evidence-driven visual reasoning pipeline for aerial grounding, covering anchor analysis, spatial relations, attribute verification, and final localization.
- Designed process- and outcome-aware GRPO rewards with a VLM-as-a-Judge evaluator, then combined multi-resolution inference and reviewer verification to improve the A-rank score from 76.4% to 77.8%.
